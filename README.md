## Music tools

### [Beat Cover & BPM](https://github.com/wuttashi1/Beat_cover-bmpbot)

Telegram toolkit for cover artwork, BPM-based timestamps and MP3 metadata. Customizable styles, reusable presets and publishing workflows.

`Python` · `Telegram` · `Pillow` · `Audio`

### [AI Factory · Telegram × n8n](https://github.com/wuttashi1/n8nstart)

Control n8n video pipelines from Telegram: upload audio and video, trigger processing and check job status.

`Python` · `n8n` · `Webhooks`

## Community tools

### [Activity Manager](https://github.com/wuttashi1/Inactivebot)

Telegram group activity management with reports, warnings, member synchronization and scheduled tasks.

`Python` · `aiogram` · `PostgreSQL`

### [Wutshy Voice Control](https://github.com/wuttashi1/dsmuterwutshy)

Discord voice-channel controls with a slash command and administrator-only Mute All and Unmute All buttons.

`Python` · `discord.py`
